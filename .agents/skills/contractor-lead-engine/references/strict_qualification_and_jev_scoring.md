# Strict Contractor Qualification & Jev Scoring Protocol

This reference defines the strict vetting rules to ensure 100% zero website false-positives and prioritize high-cashflow, social-proof-rich contractors across South Africa.

## Core Rules Summary

### 1. Tighten "No Website" Verification (Zero False Positives)
- **Facebook About / Intro:** "Website" field must be blank (or only link to Instagram/TikTok). Email must be a free provider (`@gmail.com`, `@yahoo.com`, `@outlook.com`). Any custom domain email (`info@company.co.za`) is an automatic disqualifier.
- **Google Page 1 SERP:** Search `"Business Name" + City`. Must return only Facebook, directories (Snupit, Yellow Pages), or Google Maps pin with NO "Website" button. If an owned `.co.za` or `.com` domain ranks on Page 1, disqualify immediately.

### 2. Prioritize "Social-Proof Rich, Web-Invisible" Contractors
- **Pinned Menus / Price Lists:** Pinned post with a graphic detailing services, rates, or "Call for free quote".
- **High Comment Inbound Intent:** Homeowners actively commenting on recent posts asking *"How much?"*, *"Please inbox"*, *"Do you work in [Suburb]?"*.
- **Google Business Profile without Website:** Existing Google Maps pin with 3+ reviews but no website linked.

### 3. Ability to Pay / Cash-Flow Qualifiers (Must Have 2+)
To ensure the contractor can easily afford R1,999 setup + R1,500/mo retainer without friction, look for at least two visual signals:
1. **Fleet / Branding:** Sign-written bakkies, trucks, or trailers on site.
2. **Crew & Equipment:** Team of 2+ workers in matching workwear/high-vis, scaffolding towers, TLBs, or concrete mixers.
3. **Affluent Suburb Markers:** High-end architectural homes, estate projects (Midstream, Silver Lakes, Dainfern, Zimbali), large swimming pools, or modern aluminium stacker doors.
4. **Page Longevity:** Facebook page > 12 months old with consistent posts across multiple seasons.

### 4. Photo & Asset Scraping Rules
- **Clean Graphic Logo / Avatar:** Professional graphic badge or company logo (no blurry selfies or generic clipart).
- **3–5 Hero Project Photos:** Clear, daytime, wide-angle shots of completed, beautiful work (no messy rubble, trenches, or loose pipes).
- **Owner's First Name:** Extracted from Page Transparency or post sign-offs for direct WhatsApp personalization.

### Auto-Exclude
- Day-work subcontractors seeking employment.
- Bulk building material / hardware suppliers.
- Pages inactive for > 60 days.
- Low-resolution or dark photos.
