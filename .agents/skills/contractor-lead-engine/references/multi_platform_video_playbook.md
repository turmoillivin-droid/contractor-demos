# Multi-Platform Video Playbook: TikTok, Instagram Reels & YouTube Shorts

> **Goal:** Turn 15–30 second raw contractor job-site clips into viral, lead-generating short-form videos across TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube Shorts.

---

## The 3 Viral Formats for Trade Contractors

### Format 1: "The Price Breakdown" (Highest Viral Lead Driver in SA)
*Homeowners are hungry to know real costs. Answering this upfront builds massive trust.*

* **Visual:** Smooth pan of the finished work (e.g. freshly tiled 50sqm living room, clean perimeter wall, waterproofed roof).
* **On-Screen Hook (First 2 seconds):**  
  `"How much did this 50sqm porcelain tiling cost in Centurion? 🇿🇦"`
* **Voiceover / Caption Script:**  
  *"Client wanted large-format 60x60 grey porcelain tiles throughout their lounge and hallway in Centurion.  
  - Old carpets removed & floor screed leveled: 1 day  
  - Tile laying & laser alignment: 2 days  
  - Waterproof grouting & skirting: 1 day  
  Total turnaround: 4 days.  
  Labor quote: R7,500.  
  Need your home tiled before the weekend? Tap the link in bio to get an instant WhatsApp quote directly on your phone."*

---

### Format 2: "The 3-Second Transformation" (The Aesthetic Closer)
*Perfect for Instagram Reels & YouTube Shorts to attract affluent estate homeowners.*

* **Visual Beat 1 (0:00 - 0:02):** Rapid close-up of ugly, cracked plaster, uneven tiles, or peeling exterior paint.  
  *Text:* `"Wait for the finish... 🛠️"`
* **Visual Beat 2 (0:02 - 0:15):** Snap transition on the beat of a trending audio track to the crisp, newly painted wall or laser-straight tile line.
* **Audio:** Trending aesthetic or ambient house beat.
* **On-Screen Tag:** `📍 Bryanston, Sandton | Molise Construction & Tiling`
* **Caption Call-to-Action:**  
  *"Clean lines, zero mess. All work personally supervised from foundation to finish. WhatsApp 063 255 0626 for free site visits in Sandton & Randburg."*

---

### Format 3: "Satisfying Trade ASMR" (Zero Talking Required)
*Contractors who are camera-shy love this format. No voice needed; the visual craft speaks for itself.*

* **Visual:** Extreme close-ups with crisp natural sound:
  - Trowel scraping wet cement onto a brick.
  - Laser level line glowing red across porcelain tiles.
  - Grout float smoothing grout into seamless joints.
  - Roller laying rich coat of paint onto raw plaster.
* **Text Overlay:**  
  `"Precision floor tiling in Pretoria East. WhatsApp 063 255 0626 for bookings."`
* **Why it works:** Millions of people watch trade ASMR videos for stress relief, and nearby homeowners bookmark the creator for their next renovation.

## Platform-Specific Specifications & Algorithms

### 1. TikTok (South Africa)
- **Primary Goal:** Discovery & Viral WhatsApp Inbounds.
- **Posting Time:** 12:00 PM – 1:30 PM (lunch break) & 6:30 PM – 8:30 PM (home relaxation).
- **Audio:** Use popular local sounds or original trade sound (tools working).
- **Hashtags (Use 4–6 targeted tags):**  
  `#satiktok #southafricabuilding #joburgrenovations #tilinghacks #centurion`

### 2. Instagram Reels
- **Primary Goal:** Aesthetic Verification & Trust for High-Ticket Quotes.
- **Visuals:** High-contrast, sharp lighting, 1080x1920 (9:16).
- **Location Tag:** Always tag the specific suburb (e.g. *Sandton*, *Ballito*, *Camps Bay*, *Pretoria East*).
- **Bio Link:** Must point directly to the 1-page website with the WhatsApp calculator.

### 3. YouTube Shorts
- **Primary Goal:** Long-Term Search Discoverability on Google.
- **Title Formula:**  
  `Bathroom Tiling Transformation in Centurion (Cost Breakdown) #Shorts`
- **Pinned Comment:** Direct WhatsApp quote link:  
  `💬 Need a quote in Gauteng? WhatsApp Maiketso directly: https://wa.me/27632550626`
