## Posting Best Practices (Safety & Conversion)
1. **Never blast all groups at once:** Post to 3–4 groups in the morning (7:00 AM – 8:30 AM) and 3–4 in the evening (5:30 PM – 7:00 PM).
2. **Safe Delay:** Insert a 4-to-8 minute random pause between posts.
3. **Caption Rotation:** Use 3 distinct copy variations highlighting different services (e.g. one focused on Tiling, one on Plastering, one on General Building).
4. **Source Tagging:** Always append a suburb tag to the WhatsApp URL:  
   `https://wa.me/27632550626?text=Hi%20Maiketso,%20saw%20your%20post%20in%20the%20[Suburb]%20group`
