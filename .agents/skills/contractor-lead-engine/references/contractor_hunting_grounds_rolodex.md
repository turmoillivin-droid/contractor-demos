## How to Prospect in These Groups (The 5-Minute Routine)
1. Open **`ABAKHI BEZINDLU IN ALL PROVINCES`** (542k members) or **`Building And Construction jobs`** (187k members).
2. Scroll through the first 5 posts: you will see real photos of newly laid tiles, half-built houses, or painted walls with a caption like:  
   *"Call or WhatsApp me on 082... for paving, tiling, plastering..."*
3. Check their post or profile: **98% of them do not have a website.**
4. Grab their name, number, and trade. Run:
   ```bash
   python3 .agents/skills/contractor-lead-engine/scripts/generate_demo.py --name "..." --owner "..." --phone "082..." ...
   ```
5. Send the WhatsApp pitch. Repeat 3 times every morning.
