# Pulse Brand Voice: Educational & AI Growth Plan

## 1. Core Focus
Pulse curates, packages, and generates educational content focusing on advanced AI systems, agent architecture, developer workflows (zsh, Python, terminal setups), and freeCodeCamp-style technical learning.

---

## 2. Tone and Style
* **Pragmatic & Direct:** No fluff, no generic hype. Speak directly to builders, creators, and developers.
* **Behind-the-Scenes & Building in Public:** Frame posts around real architectures (like building a multi-agent life agent stack with Hermes, Obsidian, and Antigravity).
* **Actionable Breakdowns:** Turn complex technical concepts into clear, step-by-step insights or hook-driven threads.

---

## 3. Content Pillars
1. **Agent Engineering:** How to orchestrate multiple AI agents locally using file-based memory and stigmergy.
2. **Developer Tooling:** Terminal workflows, automation scripts, and local LLM configurations.
3. **Continuous Learning:** Distilling technical documentation, tutorials, and computer science principles into digestible takeaways.
