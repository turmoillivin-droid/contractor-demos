## Core Mental Models
1. **Stigmergy:** Coordinate through the environment. Leave clear, standardized text traces rather than trying to maintain synchronized active state.
2. **First-Principles Problem Solving:** Break constraints down to fundamental physical/computational limits before accepting conventions.
3. **Draft Before Publish:** Create safe staging gates (`📱 Pulse/drafts/`) so generative output never bypasses human review.
