# ⏱ Daily Operating Routine (1.5 - 2 Hours)

Execute this exact SOP every business day to acquire contractor clients and run retainers with zero stress.

---

## 🌅 Morning Sprint: 08:00 - 08:45 (Prospecting)
1. Open [[Contractor Hunting Grounds (1.2M+)]] (specifically *ABAKHI BEZINDLU* 542k or *Building And Construction jobs* 187k).
2. Filter by **Most Recent** posts.
3. Apply the **5 Goldilocks Filters**:
   - High-ticket trade (Tiler, Roofer, Bricklayer, Builder).
   - Posted within last 24 hours.
   - Shows real project photos.
   - Has clear WhatsApp cell number.
   - Has NO website.
4. Pick **3 contractors**.
5. Run the CLI generator (takes 30 seconds):
   ```bash
   python3 .agents/skills/contractor-lead-engine/scripts/generate_demo.py \
     --name "Contractor Name" \
     --owner "Owner Name" \
     --phone "082..." \
     --city "City" \
     --province "Province" \
     --services "Service 1, Service 2, Service 3" \
     --output "demo-contractor/index.html"
   ```
6. Drop folder into [Netlify Drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop) to get a live URL.
7. Send the [[WhatsApp Outreach Scripts]] pitch with the live link.

---

## 🥪 Midday Block: 12:00 - 12:45 (Client Retainer Posting)
1. For each active paying client on the R1,500/mo retainer:
2. Select 4–5 groups from [[Gauteng Homeowner Groups (1.5M+)]] (or their local province in [[National 9-Province Directory (5.4M+)]]).
3. Post their flyer + caption with a suburb-tagged WhatsApp link:
   `https://wa.me/27...?text=Hi,%20saw%20your%20post%20in%20the%20[Suburb]%20group`
4. Space posts out with a 4-to-8 minute pause.

---

## ☕ Afternoon: 16:30 - 17:00 (Closing & Payments)
1. Open WhatsApp and check contractor replies.
2. When they ask: *"How much does it cost to keep the site?"*
   - Reply: *"It's R1,500 once-off. I hook up your Google Maps profile and keep it hosted for you. We can activate it today."*
3. Send Capitec Pay / FNB / Standard Bank details.
4. Move lead in [[Pipeline Tracker]] to `#status/closed`.

---

## 📅 Friday Afternoon: 16:00 (Client Retention)
- Take 3 screenshots of your clients' ads running live in the groups.
- Send them on WhatsApp:  
  *"Happy Friday [Name]! Here are your ad posts live in the groups this week. Enjoy your weekend!"*
- **Result:** Retains clients for 6+ months on recurring monthly retainers.
