- [ ] **Morning (30 mins):** Pick 3 fresh contractors from [[Contractor Hunting Grounds (1.2M+)]].

- [ ] **Outreach (10 mins):** Send 3 personalized WhatsApp pitches with live preview links.
- [ ] **Midday (30 mins):** Post paying clients' flyers to 4–5 community groups.
- [ ] **Afternoon (15 mins):** Reply to inquiries & collect payments (EFT / Capitec Pay).
